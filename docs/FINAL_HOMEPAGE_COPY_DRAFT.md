# 10. Implementation Notes

- Keep Atlas as the only product receiving deep copy and proof treatment.
- Do not introduce equal-weight future product cards with stronger copy than this draft.
- Do not expand the hero supporting copy beyond two sentences.
- Keep the expansion areas lower-emphasis than Atlas in both layout and contrast.
- If a direct contact email is added later, place it in the footer or contact page rather than increasing homepage copy density.
